The Vandalism — March 28, 2026

Incident Timeline Primary Incident
Approx. 6:00 PM
Last confirmed time Leake was in or near his vehicle. The 2023 BMW M8 Competition was parked outside the rental property at 302 W Colter St while the birthday party was underway inside.
Approx. 9:25 PM
As the party concluded and cleanup was nearly complete, Leake and Schilling exited the property and discovered the vehicle had been vandalized.
Chemical Damage Discovered
Sunnyside Quality 2 Minute Remover Advanced — a fast-acting industrial paint stripping compound — had been poured across the vehicle's hood, roof, front bumper, and passenger-side fender. This product is specifically formulated to dissolve automotive paint and protective coatings within minutes of contact, causing irreversible damage to both the paint and any underlying protective film.
Food Waste Applied
Cat food and Tostitos cheese product had also been poured or spread across the vehicle. Containers from both products were left at the scene — key physical evidence retained by Leake.
Tires Confirmed Punctured
The vehicle's tire pressure monitoring system showed both front tires at 2 PSI — critically below the safe operating range of 32–36 PSI. Leake attempted to reinflate both tires using a mobile emergency pump. Both tires failed to hold air and immediately deflated, confirming that the tires had been intentionally punctured. A tow truck was called.
Immediate Response
Understanding that the paint stripper would cause escalating damage the longer it remained on the surface, Leake, Schilling, roommate Bladen France, and France's girlfriend Abigail Vasquez immediately began hosing down the vehicle and manually removing compromised sections of paint protection film. This process took approximately two hours.
Missing Evidence
During the cleanup, the paint stripper can — which had been left at the scene by the perpetrators — disappeared. The group searched the property but could not locate it. The Tostitos cheese container and the lid from the paint stripper were recovered and retained as physical evidence.

Background & Context

Relationship History Context

Garrett Leake, the owner of the vandalized vehicle, previously dated Brielle Evangeline Quarmby for approximately five years. That relationship ended in April 2025. Following the breakup, Leake entered into a new relationship with Ariana Schilling.

On March 28, 2026, Schilling hosted a private birthday pool party at 302 W Colter St, Phoenix, Arizona 85013, a property she had rented through a backyard rental application. Critically, the address of this property was never posted publicly — it was shared only through private messages to specifically invited guests. Neither Brielle Quarmby nor Zamar Amaru Nasir-James was invited to this event.

Leake later learned that Quarmby had obtained the party address by accessing screenshots of Instagram direct messages belonging to one of Schilling's friends — a deliberate act of obtaining a private address without authorization. This explains how the suspects were able to locate the vehicle.

Prior Threats — March 27, 2026

Documented Threatening Contact Night Before

The evening before the vandalism, Leake and Schilling attended a concert venue where they observed that Quarmby and Nasir-James were also in attendance. The two parties did not speak and no confrontation took place at the venue.

At approximately 10:15 p.m., after Leake and Schilling left the venue, Leake began receiving a series of unsolicited phone calls. Two calls originated from the number (602) 525-5074 (Zamar Amaru Nasir-James) and one from a No Caller ID number — all within a short period of time. Leake also had prior contact from Quarmby's number (480) 369-2165 in the days surrounding the incident.

When Schilling answered one of the calls, the male caller identified himself as Zamar Amaru Nasir-James and made explicit threatening statements, stating his intention to fight Leake and demanding that Leake return to the parking lot. Schilling indicated he may have had the wrong number, but Nasir-James persisted, continuing to call multiple times.

Leake eventually answered through his car's Bluetooth screen. When he asked Nasir-James why he wanted to fight, Nasir-James stated:

"Because Brielle said it was a free fade."
— Zamar Amaru Nasir-James, March 27, 2026 — overheard by both Leake and Schilling via car Bluetooth

This statement, made in Schilling's presence and through a recorded vehicle Bluetooth system, directly establishes that Nasir-James was acting at Quarmby's direction. Following this call, Nasir-James continued attempting contact via additional No Caller ID calls and sent a text message reading "Send the lo" — street slang requesting the location. Leake did not respond and blocked the number.

In a separate scene, one individual appears to be crouched down near the driver-side front tire while the other stands nearby — consistent with the act of puncturing a tire.
